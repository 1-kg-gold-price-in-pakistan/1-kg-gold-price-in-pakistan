Gold Market Analysis & Trends for 2025

The gold market in Pakistan is influenced by a myriad of factors: global economic uncertainty, inflation, currency devaluation, and local demand during wedding seasons and festivals. In 2024, gold prices in Pakistan surged by nearly 25% due to the rupee's depreciation against the USD and rising international gold prices. As we move through 2025, experts predict continued volatility but with an upward bias.

Key Drivers Shaping Gold Prices

  • USD/PKR Exchange Rate: A weaker rupee makes gold more expensive in local currency.
  • International Gold Prices: Geopolitical tensions and central bank policies in the US impact global rates.
  • Local Demand: Wedding season (Oct-Feb) and Eid festivities drive up physical demand.
  • Inflation: Gold is a traditional hedge against inflation, boosting investment demand.
  • Government Policies: Import duties and taxes directly affect the final price.
